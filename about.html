The story

During the COVID lockdown, Samuel used his smartphone to attend free online trainings, then began teaching what he learned through WhatsApp classes and Zoom sessions. That teaching grew into mentoring, income that supports his family, and opportunities he never applied for.

Today he works as a Data Analyst skilled in Excel, SQL and Power BI, runs training cohorts and a business community from his phone, and teaches the principle he lived first.

He launched 1 Phone, 2 Thrones on YouTube in August 2026.

One phone can build your future and serve God's Kingdom at the same time.

What "two thrones" means

Your future

Skills, income and opportunity. Building a life and supporting the people who depend on you.

God's Kingdom

Service, mentoring and impact. Using what you have to lift other people.

The formula behind every lesson, talk and challenge day: Digital Skills + Diligence + Consistency = Freedom.
