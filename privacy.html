Who we are

1 Phone, 2 Thrones is a project of Samuel Ajagbe. For anything about your information, contact: onephonetwothrones@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Newsletter list: first name, email, the page you signed up on, and a record of your consent (date and wording version). Used to send new-video emails and news about events.
  • 30-day challenge: first name, email, optional WhatsApp number, how you heard about us, consent record, and your progress through the 30 days. Used to send the challenge emails and to manage your place.
  • Event registration: name, email, optional WhatsApp number, ticket code, payment status and check-in. Used to issue your ticket, send reminders and run the event.
  • Speaking invitations and contact messages: what you type in the form, including your name, email, organisation and event details. Used to reply to you.

We do not ask for passwords or card numbers. Paid events currently use bank transfer, so card details never pass through this site.

Our legal basis

For emails, your consent, which you give by ticking the box. For tickets and replies to your messages, to do what you asked us to do. You can withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing.

Who handles your information

We use these services to run the site. They process your information for us:

  • Google (Google Sheets and Apps Script) stores the records above, and Gmail sends some emails.
  • Brevo sends the challenge and newsletter emails and processes unsubscribes and bounces.
  • Vercel hosts this website and receives your IP address when you visit, as any host does.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) is contacted only after you press play on a video.
  • A QR-code image service (api.qrserver.com) generates the QR code on event tickets from the ticket link.

We do not sell your information. Some of these services are based outside Nigeria, so your information may be processed in other countries.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set its own cookies and currently runs no analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we will update this page first. Third-party services above may set their own cookies, for example YouTube once you play a video.

How long we keep it

Newsletter and challenge records are kept until you unsubscribe, and for 12 months after that so we can honour your request and avoid emailing you again. Speaking invitations and contact messages are kept for 24 months. Event registrations are kept for 24 months after the event. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from any email using the link in it.
  • Email us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
  • You may also complain to the data-protection authority in your country.

Children

The challenge, newsletter and event registrations are meant for people aged 16 and over. If you believe someone younger has given us their details, email us and we will remove them.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and change the date at the top.
