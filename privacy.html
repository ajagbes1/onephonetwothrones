Privacy Policy

Who we are

1 Phone, 2 Thrones is run by Samuel Ajagbe in Nigeria. Samuel decides what information is collected and why, so he is the data controller. For anything about your information, including using any of your rights below, email onephonetwothrones@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

This policy is for adults. You must be 18 or over to use the forms on this site.

What we collect, why, and our legal basis

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) we must have a lawful basis for every use of your information. Here it is for each purpose:

  • Newsletter: first name, email, the page you signed up on, and a record of your consent (date and wording version). Used to send new-video emails and news about events. Basis: your consent.
  • Throne Check quiz: the quiz runs in your browser and your answers are not sent to us. Only if you choose to sign up for emails after it, we store your result band and score (for example, “The Divided Throne, 17/30”) with your sign-up. Seeing your full breakdown needs no sign-up. Basis: your consent.
  • 30-day challenge: first name, email, optional WhatsApp number, how you heard about us, your consent record, and your progress through the 30 days. Used to send the challenge emails and manage your place. Basis: your consent.
  • Event registration: name, email, optional WhatsApp number, ticket code, payment status and check-in. Used to issue your ticket, send reminders and run the event. Basis: performing what you asked for (your registration). Sending you future videos and events is separate and optional. Basis: your consent.
  • Speaking invitations, mentorship and contact messages: what you type, including your name, email, optional phone, organisation and event details. Used to reply to you. Basis: taking steps at your request before an agreement, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.
  • Records that you unsubscribed: we keep your email on a do-not-email list for the period below so you are not emailed again by mistake. Basis: our legitimate interest in honouring your choice.

WhatsApp number (optional). We use it only to reach you about your challenge place or the event you registered for, for example if an email does not reach you or a time or venue changes. We do not add you to groups or send marketing messages on WhatsApp.

We do not ask for passwords or card numbers. Paid events currently use bank transfer, so card details never pass through this site.

Religious belief

The challenge, videos and newsletter are based on Christian faith. Signing up for them may therefore reveal your religious belief, which the NDPA treats as sensitive personal data. We only process this because you ticked the consent box that says so. We do not ask about your beliefs, do not record them separately, and do not use them for anything except sending you the content you signed up for. You can say no by not signing up, and you can withdraw at any time.

Who handles your information

We use these services to run the site. They process your information for us, under their data-processing terms:

  • Google (Google Sheets and Apps Script) stores the records above, and Gmail sends some emails.
  • Brevo sends the challenge and newsletter emails and processes unsubscribes and bounces.
  • Vercel hosts this website and receives your IP address when you visit, as any host does.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) is contacted when the Videos page opens, because the featured video starts there, and on other pages only after you press play.

We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Sending information outside Nigeria

These services run in other countries, such as the United States and countries in the European Union. When your information goes there, we rely on the provider’s contractual data-protection commitments, and on your consent where you give it by submitting a form. We send only what is needed.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set its own cookies and currently runs no analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we will update this page first. Third-party services above may set their own cookies, for example YouTube once you play a video.

How long we keep it

  • Newsletter and challenge records: while you stay subscribed or in the challenge, and for 12 months after you unsubscribe or the sign-up expires, so we can honour your request and avoid emailing you again.
  • Speaking invitations, mentorship and contact messages: 24 months from when we received them.
  • Event registrations: 24 months after the event.

After that we delete the records. You can ask us to delete yours sooner.

Keeping it safe

The forms send information over an encrypted connection. Records sit in a Google account protected by the owner’s sign-in, and only Samuel has access to the records. The forms are protected against automated abuse, and entries are cleaned before they are stored. If a data breach puts your rights at serious risk, we will tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and tell you without undue delay.

Your rights

You can email us at any time to:

  • ask whether we hold information about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • have your information deleted, or its use restricted;
  • withdraw your consent. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before you withdrew. The easiest way for emails is the unsubscribe link in any email;
  • object to us using your information, including for direct marketing;
  • receive your information in a common format where the law provides for that.

We make no decisions about you by computer alone that have legal or similarly significant effect. The quiz result is shown to you for guidance and nothing more.

You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you think we have mishandled your information. We would appreciate the chance to put it right first, so please email us.

Children

This site is for people aged 18 and over. The forms ask you to confirm it. If you believe someone younger has given us their details, email us and we will delete them.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your information in a way you agreed to, we will ask you again.
