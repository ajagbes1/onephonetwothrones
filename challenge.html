FAQ

Questions about the challenge

More questions? Send us a message.

Is it free?

Yes. It costs nothing and you do not need an account.

When does the first email arrive?

Day 1 arrives the day after you confirm your email. Emails go out once a day. We cannot promise an exact minute because delivery depends on email providers.

I did not get the confirmation email. What now?

Check Promotions and spam, and move it to your main inbox. If it is not there after a few minutes, submit the form again with the same email. You can also write to onephonetwothrones@gmail.com.

How do I stop the emails?

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can leave at any time.

Do I have to be a Christian?

No. The challenge is open to anyone who is willing to take their life beyond where it is now. It is written from a Christian point of view and each day includes a scripture, so you will see that in the emails.

What happens to my details?

We use your name and email to send the challenge. See the Privacy Policy for what we store and who processes it.
