About the speaker

Samuel Ajagbe is a Data Analyst skilled in Excel, SQL and Power BI. During the COVID lockdown he learned from free online trainings on his smartphone and began teaching what he learned. That grew into mentoring, training cohorts and a business community, all run from his phone.

He speaks from experience on what one phone, used on purpose, can build.
