FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Send us a message.

Is the 30-day challenge really free?

Yes. It costs nothing to join and you do not need an account. You only need an email address.

How much time does it take each day?

About 15 minutes. Each email has one small task.

What if I miss a day?

Do not start over. Just do today's task and keep going.

Do I have to be a Christian to join?

No. The challenge is open to anyone who is willing to take their life beyond where it is now. It is written from a Christian point of view and each day includes a scripture, so you will see that in the emails.

Can I stop the emails?

Yes. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can use it at any time.

What do you do with my details?

We use your name and email to send what you signed up for. Read the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
